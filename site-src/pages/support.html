Codes & accounts

How do I redeem a code?
  • PC: open the Peakabu app and use the Redeem section, or redeem on the pricing page while signed in.
  • Mobile: sign in to Peakabu Mobile and enter your code where it asks to redeem.

If a code says it's invalid, check for extra spaces or look-alike characters (O vs 0, I vs 1). Still stuck? Email us the code and your username.

I bought a code but never got the email

Check your spam or promotions folder first. Emailing the code is optional at checkout, so if you didn't enter an email, nothing was sent.

Either way, contact us with the purchase date, the amount, and the last 4 digits of the card you used, and we'll find your code.

I forgot my password

Peakabu accounts only need a username and password, with no email on file. That means we can only recover an account that has Discord linked.

If you've linked Discord, contact us and we'll help you back in. If you haven't, we have no way to prove the account is yours. Link Discord on your account page to protect yourself.

Do PC and Mobile use the same account?

Yes. Sign in to Peakabu Mobile with the same username and password you use on PC. Squad and Pro plans include Peakabu Mobile hosting for as long as the plan runs.

Can I use Peakabu Mobile for free?

Yes. Free accounts can host one session of up to 10 minutes per day. Guests who join by QR code are always free. Paid access removes the time limit.

Billing & refunds

Can I get a refund?

Yes, on your first purchase, if you've barely used it. You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying, as long as you haven't gone past the usage limit for your plan:

PlanRefundable until you've created
Creator2 sessions
Squad4 sessions
Pro11 sessions
Mobile host (1 or 3 months)3 hosted sessions

Whichever comes first, the 14 days or the usage limit, ends the refund window. Haven't used it at all? You qualify. Renewals are not refundable. We always honor any refund rights your local consumer law gives you.

To request one, email report@peakabu.app with your username, the purchase date, the amount, and the last 4 card digits. Full details are in Terms, section 5.5.

Can I cancel an event pass?

Yes. Cancel any time before your event window starts for a full refund, even if you picked the wrong date. Email us with your username and the event date.

Once the window starts, the pass can't be refunded. If Peakabu didn't work during your event, tell us and we'll make it right.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. Every purchase is a one-time payment for a code with a fixed amount of time. Nothing renews and there's nothing to cancel. When your time runs out, buy another code if you want to keep going.

Who handles my payment?

Stripe. Peakabu never sees or stores your full card number. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what Stripe shares with us.

Recording & uploads

My upload is slow or failed
  • Keep the Peakabu app open until the upload queue finishes.
  • On a weak connection, switch to Low Bandwidth (Turtle) mode.
  • If one player's view is missing from a session, have that player check that their upload finished.

If it keeps happening, email us your capture mode (Monitor, Screen Capture, or Low Bandwidth), your Windows version, and the session link.

How long are my clips kept?

It depends on your plan: 24 hours on Free, 30 days on Creator and Squad, 60 days on Pro. Peakabu Mobile sessions are kept for 60 days. After that they're permanently deleted and can't be recovered, so download anything you want to keep.

Safety, privacy & your data

Report a session, clip, or user

Use the Report button on any session or clip, or email report@peakabu.app with the link and a short description.

If you appear in someone else's recording and want it taken down, email us the link. For copyright claims, see Terms, section 24.

Delete your account and data

Delete it yourself from your account page: scroll to Delete account, enter your password, and confirm. It covers PC, Mobile, and the website, and you have 7 days to change your mind by signing back in.

Can't sign in? Email report@peakabu.app with your username. See what gets deleted and what stays.
