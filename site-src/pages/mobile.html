Spots

Big event? Give each area its own QR.

At a wedding, a Peak at the pool shouldn't interrupt the head table. Set up Spots, print a QR card for each one, and guests join where they're standing. It all stays one session, color-coded by Spot when you watch back.

Any signed-in account can make and print cards ahead of time, free accounts included. They come alive when the party starts on a host plan or event pass.
