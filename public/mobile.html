Three taps from walking in to watching back.

01 · SCAN

Join from the QR

Guests point their camera at the host's code and they're in. Their phone keeps a short rolling buffer, never one long recording.

02 · PEAK

Anyone hits PEAK

Every filming phone saves that moment at once. A short cooldown stops spam; photos still work in between.

03 · WATCH

Every angle, together

Up to 4 synced views on a phone, 8 on tablets and bigger. Swipe to swap angles, pin comments to the moment.

Swing cards

Big event? Give each area its own QR.

At a wedding, a Peak at the pool shouldn't interrupt the head table. Print a Swing card for each area (“Swing by the Pool”) and guests join where they're standing. It all stays one session, color-coded by area when you watch back.

Any signed-in account can make and print cards ahead of time, free accounts included. They come alive when the party starts on a host plan or event pass.
