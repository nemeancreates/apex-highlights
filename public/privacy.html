3. Information You Give Us

Account information. A username and a password — no email address is required. Passwords are stored only as a bcrypt hash. Because we don't collect an email address, password recovery is only possible if you've linked a Discord account.

Linked accounts. If you connect Discord, we receive your Discord user ID, username, avatar, and (where you grant it) server membership information used to sync your roles. We do not receive your Discord password or read your messages.

Payment information. Subscriptions are processed by Stripe, which collects your billing details — including a billing email address, for receipts — directly at checkout. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe passes us a customer ID, subscription status, the card's last four digits and brand, and billing country — and, if you complete a purchase, your billing email as well, so we can link the subscription to your account and reach you about billing issues. This is the one case where the Service does collect an email address.

Redemption codes. If you redeem a crowdfunding or promotional code, we record which code was redeemed and by which account.

Support and correspondence. If you email us or report a bug, we keep the message and any logs, screenshots, or files you attach.

User-entered content. Session names, clip titles, notes, and similar text you type into the Service.

4. Your Recordings

4.1 The local buffer

While capture is active, the Peak-Abu application continuously records your selected game window and audio into a rolling local buffer on your own computer. This buffer never leaves your device unless you save a clip; older segments are continuously overwritten, and the buffer is cleared when capture stops.

4.2 What gets uploaded

When you or a Session Host saves a clip — including one saved automatically by Auto-Highlight — the captured segment is encoded and uploaded to our storage provider so it can be synced with other participants' points of view and played back in the web player. A saved clip may contain video of your selected window, your microphone audio (if enabled), your desktop/system audio (if enabled, which can include voice chat from your party), and anything else visible in the captured window. You control what's captured through the app's window and audio settings.

4.3 Other people in your recordings

Multi-POV capture inherently records other people. You are responsible for obtaining any consent required by law before recording anyone — recording laws vary by state and country. If you appear in someone else's Peak-Abu recording and want it removed, contact peakabullc@gmail.com; we will work with the account holder to address the request.

4.4 We do not watch your clips

We do not review, monitor, or manually inspect your recordings, except when you explicitly send us a clip for support, when responding to a specific abuse report or legal request, or when required by law.

4.5 Automated processing

Where enabled at your tier, the Service may analyze clips to generate highlight reels, previews, or thumbnails. Depending on the feature and tier, this runs on your own device, on our servers, or through Anthropic's Claude API. Where Claude is used, your clip data is sent to Anthropic solely to generate your output; Anthropic's commercial API terms state customer inputs and outputs are not used to train their models and are deleted from their systems within 30 days of processing. We do not perform facial recognition, voiceprint identification, or any biometric identification on your recordings.

5. Information Collected Automatically

5.1 Device and application data

  • Operating system and version, CPU/GPU information, screen resolution
  • Peak-Abu application version
  • Capture engine in use and encoder settings
  • Locale and time zone

5.2 Running application detection

To populate the capture-target picker and label session history with the game being played, the application reads the names of windows and processes running on your computer. This runs locally — we transmit only the identified game name (e.g. "Apex Legends") to our servers, not a full list of your running applications. We do not read the contents of other applications, your files, your keystrokes, or your clipboard.

5.3 Connection and security data

  • IP address and approximate region derived from it
  • Login timestamps, success/failure, and account identifiers
  • Socket connection events and session join/leave activity
  • Rate-limiting and anomaly-detection signals used to detect credential stuffing, abuse, and quota circumvention

5.4 Crash and error reports

We use Sentry for automated crash and error reporting on both the desktop application and our server. A report may include the error and stack trace, application state at time of failure, OS/device/app version, file paths (which on Windows commonly contain your account name), session and clip identifiers, and your Peak-Abu user ID where the error occurred while signed in. We configure Sentry not to capture authentication tokens or passwords.

5.5 Update checks

The application periodically contacts our servers to check for updates, transmitting its current version and platform.

5.6 Website and web player

We do not run analytics or advertising scripts on peakabu.app or in the web player, and we set no cookies of our own. The site and player use browser local storage only for interface preferences — your selected theme, panel layout, comment display settings, and which clips you've already viewed — which stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. Authentication in the web player uses a bearer token held in memory for the session, not a cookie.

Some pages embed YouTube videos. Loading an embedded video can allow YouTube (Google) to set its own cookies and collect data under Google's privacy policy, independent of anything Peak-Abu does. We do not control this third-party behavior.

6. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an email address to create an account
  • We do not capture your screen outside the window or region you've selected
  • We do not upload buffered footage — only clips you or Auto-Highlight have saved
  • We do not log keystrokes, read your clipboard, or scan your files
  • We do not read the contents of other running applications
  • We do not collect precise geolocation
  • We do not perform facial or voice recognition
  • We do not sell or rent personal information
  • We do not serve advertising or share data with ad networks
  • We do not train AI models on your recordings

10. Share Links Are Public By Design

Anyone with a Session's share link can watch that Session's clips in the web player without an account. Share links use long, randomly generated identifiers that are impractical to guess, but they are not secret, and once shared you cannot control who it gets forwarded to. Clips remain reachable by link until they expire under your tier's retention period. Treat anything you capture and share as potentially public.

11. Security

  • HTTPS/TLS for all traffic between the application, the web player, and our servers
  • bcrypt password hashing with a work factor of 12
  • JWT bearer tokens with server-side token versioning so credentials can be invalidated
  • OS-level encryption of stored tokens on your device via Electron's safeStorage
  • Server-side authorization checks on every request
  • Content Security Policy and origin restrictions on the web player
  • Secrets held in environment variables, never in source code
  • Automated anomaly detection on authentication and usage patterns
  • SSH key–only access to production servers

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and any required regulators within the timeframes the law requires. To report a vulnerability, email peakabullc@gmail.com.
