How it works

Four steps. The squad does one of them.

01HOST

Start a session

One player opens Peakabu and gets a session code.

02SQUAD

Squad joins

Everyone drops the same code into their client. Now you share one clock.

03AUTO

Just play

Each client keeps a rolling buffer of its own gameplay in the background, on your own PC.

04ANYONE

Hit the hotkey

Every POV of that moment saves at once and lands in the web player, synced.

Security

We see the play.* Here's how we protect it.

*Peakabu never requires your real name, email address, or birth date. An account needs only a username and password. Purchases are handled by Stripe, which collects its own billing details. See our Privacy Policy and Terms, section 4.

Hashed passwords

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored as text.

Encrypted sign-in on your PC

The desktop app keeps your sign-in encrypted with Windows' own protection.

HTTPS everywhere

All traffic is encrypted, with strict browser security headers.

Checked on our servers

Your plan and session membership are verified server-side on every request.

Uploads are inspected

Files are checked for type and size before they're stored.

Clips expire on schedule

Footage is removed automatically when your plan's storage window ends.

Private by default

Sessions stay off the public list unless the host turns it on.

Host moderation

Hosts can kick or ban anyone from their session.

Clean logs, audited code

Sensitive data is stripped from logs, and dependencies are pinned and scanned.
