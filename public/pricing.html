Questions

Do my friends need a paid plan?

No. Plans only limit what the host can do. Anyone can join a session or watch a link for free. The host's plan sets the squad size and how long clips are kept.

What happens when clips expire?

They're deleted automatically at the end of your plan's storage window. Download anything you want to keep before then.

Is Peakabu Mobile a separate account?

No, it's the same account and sign-in. Mobile hosting is its own plan, or comes free with Squad and Pro.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and your plan keeps working until the end of the time you've already paid for. After that the account moves to Free.

Can I be signed in on my PC and phone at once?

Yes. You can be signed in once on the desktop app, once on a phone and once on the website at the same time. Signing in on a second device of the same kind signs the older one out.
